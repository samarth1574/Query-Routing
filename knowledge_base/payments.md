## Pending payments
Card payments show as pending until the merchant settles them, which usually takes 1–3 working days and up to 7 days for some merchants such as hotels and car rental. Pending amounts reduce the available balance. If a pending payment is not settled after 7 days it is automatically released.

## Declined or failed transfers
Transfers can fail because of incorrect recipient details, insufficient balance, daily limits, or a security check. No money leaves the account for a failed transfer. If a transfer shows as failed but money was deducted, it is returned within 2 working days.

## Transfer timing
Faster Payments in the UK usually arrive within 2 hours and always within 1 working day. International SWIFT transfers take 1–5 working days. SEPA transfers take up to 1 working day.

## Refunds
Merchant refunds usually appear within 5–10 working days of the merchant processing them. The bank cannot speed up a merchant refund. If a refund has not appeared after 15 working days, the customer can raise a dispute.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If a transaction appears twice, one of them is often a pending authorisation that will drop off. If both settle, the customer can raise a dispute for the duplicate charge, which is normally resolved within 10 working days.

## Fees and exchange rates
Card payments abroad use the Visa or Mastercard exchange rate plus a 0.5% fee on weekends. Top-ups by debit card are free; top-ups by credit card carry a 1% fee. Cash withdrawals are free up to £200 per month, then 2%.

## Top-ups
Top-ups by bank transfer arrive within 2 hours. Card top-ups are instant. A top-up can be reverted if the card issuer rejects it; the funds are returned to the original card within 5 working days.
