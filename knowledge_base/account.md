## Identity verification
New customers must verify their identity with a passport or driving licence and a selfie in the app. Most checks complete within 10 minutes; manual checks can take up to 2 working days. Verification is required by anti-money-laundering regulations and cannot be skipped.

## Source of funds
For large or unusual deposits we may ask for proof of the source of funds, such as payslips, a sale contract, or bank statements. Accounts may be restricted until the documents are reviewed.

## Forgotten passcode
A forgotten passcode can be reset from the login screen with Forgot passcode. A reset link is sent to the registered email address and expires after 30 minutes. For security, staff will never ask for the full passcode.

## Changing personal details
Name, address, and phone number can be updated under Profile in the app. A name change requires a supporting document such as a marriage certificate or deed poll.

## Closing an account
Customers can close their account at any time in the app under Profile → Close account, once the balance is zero and there are no pending transactions or outstanding loans.

## Eligibility and supported countries
Customers must be at least 18 years old (13 for teen accounts with a parent). Accounts are available to residents of the UK and the European Economic Area.
