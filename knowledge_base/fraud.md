## Unrecognised transactions
If a customer does not recognise a transaction, first check whether it is a merchant trading under a different name or a pending authorisation. If it is still unrecognised, block the card immediately and raise a dispute. Under the Payment Services Regulations, customers are refunded for unauthorised transactions unless they acted fraudulently or with gross negligence. Disputes are normally resolved within 10 working days.

## Compromised, skimmed or cloned cards
If card details may have been copied (skimming at an ATM, a data breach, or a phishing site), the card must be blocked and replaced. All transactions in the last 30 days should be reviewed with the customer.

## Scams and authorised push payment fraud
If a customer was tricked into sending money (a scam), the Fraud & Security Team must review the case. We contact the receiving bank within 1 hour to try to recover funds. Reimbursement decisions follow the APP reimbursement rules and are made within 5 working days. These cases are always handled by a human specialist.

## Lost or stolen phone
If a phone with the banking app is lost or stolen, the customer should log out of all devices from the web portal and block their cards. The SIM should be blocked with the mobile operator to prevent SIM-swap attacks.

## Account takeover
Signs of account takeover include password changes the customer did not make, unknown devices, or being locked out. The account is frozen immediately and a fraud specialist contacts the customer by phone using the number on file.

## Security reminders
The bank will never ask for a full PIN, passcode, or one-time code by phone, email or text. Customers should never move money to a "safe account" at the request of a caller.
