## Eligibility
Personal loans are available from £1,000 to £50,000 over 12 to 84 months. Applicants must be 18 or over, UK residents, have a verified identity, a credit score of at least 600, and total monthly repayments (including the new loan) of no more than 45% of monthly income.

## Interest rates
Representative APRs: personal loans 11.9%, car loans 8.9%, home loans 6.4%, student loans 5.5%. The actual rate depends on credit score and amount. An eligibility check is a soft search and does not affect the credit score.

## Applying and application status
Applications take about 10 minutes in the app. Most decisions are instant; some need a manual underwriting review that takes up to 2 working days. Funds are paid out within 1 working day of signing the agreement.

## Repayments and EMIs
Repayments (EMIs) are collected by direct debit on the chosen date each month. A missed payment incurs a £12 fee and is reported to credit agencies after 30 days. Customers in financial difficulty should contact the Lending Team before missing a payment; payment holidays of up to 3 months may be available.

## Early repayment and closure
Loans can be repaid early at any time. An early repayment charge of up to 58 days' interest may apply. A settlement figure can be requested in the app and is valid for 28 days. A closure letter is sent within 10 working days.
