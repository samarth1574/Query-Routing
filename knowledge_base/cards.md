## Lost or stolen card
If a card is lost or stolen, block it immediately in the app under Cards → Block card, or ask the assistant to block it. Blocking is instant and stops all new card payments and ATM withdrawals. A replacement card is sent automatically and arrives in 3–5 working days. Recurring payments must be moved to the new card by the customer.

## Card not working or declined
A card payment can be declined because of insufficient funds, a blocked or frozen card, an expired card, an incorrect PIN, a merchant that does not accept the card network, or a security check. Customers should check the card status and balance in the app first. Contactless payments are limited to £100 per transaction; above that, chip and PIN is required.

## PIN blocked or forgotten
After three incorrect PIN attempts the PIN is blocked. The PIN can be viewed in the app under Cards → Show PIN, and unblocked at any of our ATMs using Change PIN. PIN changes take effect immediately at ATMs.

## Card delivery and activation
New physical cards arrive within 5 working days in the UK and up to 15 working days abroad. Cards must be activated in the app by entering the 6-digit code printed on the letter. If a card has not arrived after 10 working days, it is cancelled and a new one is sent free of charge.

## Virtual and disposable cards
Virtual cards are available instantly in the app and can be used for online payments and Apple Pay or Google Pay. Disposable virtual cards generate new details after each payment. Up to 5 virtual cards can be active at a time.

## Card swallowed by ATM
If an ATM keeps a card, block the card in the app and order a replacement. Banks cannot retrieve cards from third-party ATMs.
